# Win: Finance Requirements Leadership — November 2026

**Type:** Cross-functional requirements and collaboration | **Scope:** ERMP Finance service accruals and service agreements

## Context

As service-accrual and service-agreement work moved toward QA, I drove conversations with Finance and delivery partners to turn defect-oriented 1036X tickets into buildable requirements. I asked for operational context before extending the solution, helped align the group around a first-release boundary, and created a path for clearer ticket acceptance criteria before the next demo.

## What changed because of it

- Budget rules were defined.
- The location, event, and session hierarchy was confirmed.
- Ownership for loading agreements was settled.
- Manual spreadsheet upload was chosen for the first release, while live integration discussion moved out of scope.
- Per-ticket requirements were captured, and ticket cleanup was scheduled before the next demo.
- Nora's Jira updates to ERMP-10362 and ERMP-10363 followed my Nov. 6 request about six days later.

## My role by meeting

- **Nov. 4, Finance meeting:** Presented the fixed service-accrual work, noted that many reported 400 errors were fixed and the work was moving to QA with UAT next, and asked Finance for context missing from the 1036X tickets. Asked Sal for a one-on-one knowledge transfer. [^nov4]
- **Nov. 5, working session with Sal:** Asked about variance, staffing and travel, data sources, UKG integration, the GL account list, and the worksheet. Asked Sal to annotate and share the worksheet. [^nov5]
- **Nov. 6, Event Financials Q&A:** Asked about SAP, Salesforce, and Concur ownership; helped align on the hierarchy; proposed manual upload as the MVP; asked who could explain the pre-SAP invoice-to-payment process; pushed for definitions of done and requirements before building; raised the audit-trail need; added Sam as business approver; asked to join reporting meetings; and proposed ticket rewrites before the demo. Nora agreed. [^nov6]
- **Nov. 12, working session:** Explained the current state, proposed an accrual-override preview that preserves planner buffers, asked for service-line-screen acceptance criteria, and suggested ticket cleanup, research, and a show-and-tell the following week. [^nov12]

## Learnings

- Defect descriptions identify symptoms; Finance context and acceptance criteria identify the work worth building.
- A smaller manual MVP can reduce risk while data quality and integration ownership are still being clarified.
- Asking for requirements early creates space for audit and approval needs before implementation makes them expensive to add.
